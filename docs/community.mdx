Report bugs and request features in [GitHub issues](https://github.com/PrefectHQ/marvin/issues). Include your Marvin and Pydantic AI versions, a small reproduction, and the error you see.

Contributions are welcome through [pull requests](https://github.com/PrefectHQ/marvin/pulls). The [repository README](https://github.com/PrefectHQ/marvin#develop) has development and test commands.
